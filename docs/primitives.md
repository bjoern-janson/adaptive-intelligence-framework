# Adaptive Intelligence Primitives

This repository is organized around a small set of primitives rather than algorithms.

These primitives are intended to be substrate-independent.

---

## Reality

External consequences that exist independently of the adaptive system.

Reality is the source of corrective pressure.

---

## Correction

The process by which empirical consequences modify future behavior.

Core quantities:

- Θ — Reality permeability
- C — Correction capacity

Adaptive systems remain stable only while correction can keep pace with adaptive expansion.

---

## Representation

Internal coordinate system used to organize observations.

When residual error cannot be reduced through parameter optimization alone, representation must expand.

Observable:

ΔR > 0

---

## Mechanism

The currently active adaptation process.

Mechanisms update states while remaining inside a fixed adaptive process.

---

## Mechanism Generator

The process responsible for generating or selecting future adaptive mechanisms.

A transition occurs when the system begins modifying how it adapts rather than only what it adapts.

---

## Adaptive Capacity

Adaptive capacity measures how effectively feedback is converted into future viability.

I ∝ C_improve

where

C_improve = capacity to convert feedback into increased future viability

---

## Vertical Stack

Reality
↓
Correction
↓
Representation
↓
Mechanism
↓
Mechanism Generator

The remainder of the framework specializes one or more layers of this stack.
